  SOULS DEATH COUNTER - COMPANION HOTKEY APP
  100% Free & Open-Source (MIT License)
=====================================================

QUICK START:
1. Double-click "RunHotkeys.bat" (or "DeathCounterHotkeys.exe").
2. Sign in with Google or connect your Streamer Key (sign-in is mandatory).
3. Switch into your Souls game and play!

DEFAULT IN-GAME SHORTCUTS:
  [F8] -> +1 Death (plays sound effect & updates overlay)
  [F7] -> Undo Last Death

CUSTOMIZING SHORTCUTS & SERVER:
Open "hotkey-config.json" in Notepad to customize your hotkeys or 
link to your own deployed server URL.

WHY DOES WINDOWS SHOW A SMART-SCREEN WARNING?
Newly downloaded .exe files from the web receive an automatic "Internet Zone" 
marker from your browser until thousands of global downloads accumulate.
- Running "RunHotkeys.bat" automatically unblocks the files for you!
- Or: Right-click DeathCounterHotkeys.exe -> Properties -> check "Unblock" -> OK.
- Or: Click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
